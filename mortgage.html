To use the Home Mortgage Calculator, enter the amount of the mortgage, the interest of the loan, and the number of years. Then click on the "Calculate" button to see an estimate of your monthly payment.

How the calculator works: The script reads your mortgage amount, interest rate, and number of years. It divides the annual rate by 1,200 to get a monthly rate, multiplies years by 12 for total payments, then applies the standard amortization formula: Payment = Principal × (monthlyRate / (1 − (1 + monthlyRate)^−n)). The result is formatted with dollar signs and commas automatically.
