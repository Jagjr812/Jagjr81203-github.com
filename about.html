About Colonial

A tradition of authentic Spanish cuisine since 1987.

Our Story

Colonial was founded in Miami in 1987 by the Reyes family, who brought the bold, soulful flavors of traditional Spanish and Cuban cooking to the heart of the city. What began as a small family kitchen has grown into a beloved community gathering place, celebrated for its warm atmosphere and unforgettable food.

Our Philosophy

We believe great food starts with great ingredients. Every dish on our menu is prepared fresh daily using locally sourced produce, imported Spanish spices, and recipes passed down through generations. From our hand-rolled tapas to our slow-braised entrées, every plate tells a story.

Our Team

Our kitchen is led by Executive Chef Maria Reyes, whose classical training in Barcelona and lifelong passion for her family's culinary heritage shape every menu we create. Her team shares that same dedication to craft, quality, and hospitality.
